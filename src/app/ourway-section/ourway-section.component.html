Our process

Five planets. One launch.

Every project follows the same five phases. What changes is how deep we go on each. Here's the whole arc in plain language.

01 · Discovery

Questions first

We start with what you're actually trying to accomplish, and whether software is even the right answer.

02 · Design

Sketch & system

Wireframes before pixels. We design the user flow before we design the screenshot.

03 · Build

Two-week loops

Real, deployed progress every check-in. No "it'll be done when it's done." You see it move.

04 · Launch

Soft, then loud

Test, fix, soft-launch, watch. We don't hand over a URL and vanish on launch day.

05 · Orbit

We stick around

Most of our best work happens in the first year after launch. We're there for it.
