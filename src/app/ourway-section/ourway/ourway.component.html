01 · Discovery

Questions first

Planets don't just appear. They form from chaos and debris. We start by figuring out what you're actually orbiting around. Half the time the original idea shifts. That's the job.

02 · Design

Sketch & system

Maps before missions. We chart the flow before we give it atmosphere. A polished screenshot nobody can navigate is just a gas giant. Looks impressive, impossible to land on.

03 · Build

Two-week loops

We don't disappear into a development nebula for months. Real deployed progress every check-in, no "it'll be done when it's done." The orbit is visible from the ground.

04 · Launch

Soft, then loud

Re-entry is the most dangerous part of any mission. We test, fix, and soft-launch before amplifying. We don't hand you the heat shield and wave goodbye from orbit.

05 · Orbit

We stick around

The moon's been at this for 4.5 billion years. We're not that committed, but we stay close. Most of our best work happens in the first year after launch, when it starts to matter.
